Privacy notice

Last updated 17 November 2026

This notice says what personal details we collect when you ask us to check or plan a pet move, why we need them, who sees them and how long we keep them.

Who we are

Fugr International Sdn. Bhd. (202601033028), 29-02, Jalan Aliff 6, Taman Damansara Aliff, 81200 Johor Bahru, Johor, Malaysia. We trade as FUGR.

What we collect

  • Your name, phone or WhatsApp number and email address
  • Your home address, where we need it to collect or deliver your pet or to complete a permit
  • Your flight details, so we can check the airline and route rules for your pet
  • Your pet's details and records: species, breed, age, microchip number, vaccination and health records

You give us these yourself, on WhatsApp or by email. If you choose not to give them, we may not be able to check or plan your pet's move.

This website

The form on this website does not send or store anything. It writes a message in your own WhatsApp or email, and you decide whether to send it.

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and button presses on this website. It does not receive what you type in the form.

Why we use your details

  • To check whether your pet can travel in time, and to tell you the result
  • To plan and carry out the move you ask us to handle
  • To prepare your quote and take payment
  • To keep the records the law requires of us

Who we share them with

We share only what each one needs to do its part of your pet's move:

  • Vets and laboratories that examine, treat or test your pet
  • Government authorities in Malaysia and in the country your pet is leaving or entering, for permits, inspection and quarantine
  • Airlines and their cargo handlers, and transport partners who move your pet

Some of these are outside Malaysia, because your pet is travelling there. We do not sell your details to anyone.

How long we keep them

We keep your records for 7 years after your pet's move is finished. After that we delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, to correct them, or to stop using them. Write to info@fugr.com.my. If we stop using details that a move depends on, we may not be able to finish that move.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we will put the new version on this page with a new date.
